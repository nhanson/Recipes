# Kahlua Bars
*From Portland's Palate*  
*LOOKS LIKE A SIMPLE CHOCOLATE BROWNIE*  
*UNTIL YOU TAKE YOUR FIRST BITE!*  

## Ingredients
4 ounces unsweetened chocolate  
&frac23; cup butter, room temperature  
2 cups granulated sugar  
4 eggs  
1 teaspoon vanilla extract  
&frac12; cup Kahlua  
1&frac14; cups all-purpose flour  
1 teaspoon baking powder  
1 teaspoon salt  

#### Frosting:
2 tablespoons Kahlua  
1 teaspoon instant coffee  
&frac12; cup butter (1 cube), room temperature  
2 cups powdered sugar  
&frac12; teaspoon vanilla extract  
2 to 3 teaspoons milk  

#### Topping:
1 ounce semi-sweet chocolate  
1 tablespoon butter  

## Instructions
Preheat oven to 350&deg;F (177&deg;C)  
Grease a 9&times;13-inch baking pan  
In a medium saucepan over low heat, melt the chocolate and butter together  
Remove pan from heat and mix in sugar, eggs, vanilla and Kahlua  
Stir in flour, baking powder and salt  
Spread in prepared pan  
Bake at 350 degrees, 25 to 30 minutes or until batter starts to pull away from sides of pan  
Cool  

#### FROSTING:
In a small bowl, mix Kahlua with instant coffee, stirring until coffee is dissolved  
In a medium bowl, cream butter, powdered sugar and vanilla together  
Add coffee mixture and beat until well blended  
Add milk to desired consistency  
Spread frosting over cooled brownies  

#### TOPPING:
In a small saucepan, melt chocolate and butter together  
Cool slightly, then drizzle topping over frosting  
Cool until chocolate is set  
Cut into bars  

### *Makes 24 to 32 bars*
